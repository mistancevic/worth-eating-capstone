One ordinary Tuesday, drawn from the food tables the prototype actually uses. Nothing here is a bad decision. The day simply spends him, and the only moment he is free to choose is the moment he has the least left to choose with.

Where it breaks

before 06:40 · and every day since

The number he is measured against is in a PDF he has not opened

His coach set 150 g of protein on day one and sent it as an attachment. Tom read it once. He could not tell you the figure now, and nothing in his day has ever asked him to.

Why it matters: a target nobody can recall is not a target, it is a document. Every downstream failure inherits from this one.
17:45

Everyone else eats first, and he eats what is left over

The children are fed at a quarter to six because they will not wait. He picks at the pasta while clearing plates. That is 11.6 g of protein and he would not describe it as having eaten.

Why it matters: by his turn there is no attention left for a decision, only for whatever is at eye level in the fridge.
21:35

What he reaches for does not register as a feeding

Bread and forty grams of Gouda carries 13.5 g. That is half of the 26 g a meal has to carry before the body treats it as one. He has eaten, he feels he has eaten, and the day has moved from 33 g to 46 g.

Why it matters: this is the failure that hides. It does not feel like skipping a meal, because he did not skip a meal.
23:05, then 02:00, then tomorrow

He is hungry again at eleven, and pays for it twice

A thousand calories does not get a 34-year-old through the night. He sleeps badly, drinks five coffees the next day, loses his appetite by lunchtime, and arrives at the same fridge in the same state.

Why it matters: the loop is self-feeding. Nothing in it corrects on its own.
roughly six weeks later

The coach is the last person to find out

He learns about it when the weight and the strength stop moving. By then it has been happening most nights for a month and a half, and neither of them can say which nights.

Why it matters: the slowest feedback loop we could have designed. The information exists at 21:20 and arrives in April.

Where the agent gets to stand

21:20 · the flat is quiet, the fridge is open

One moment, one question, one thing to add

This is the only point in the day where Tom is free, awake enough to type a sentence, and standing in front of the food. It is also the last point at which anything can still be changed. Everything earlier belongs to his job and his children. Everything later is already the bill.

The agent gets 33 g on the board, 1,504 kcal of room, and a fridge with three things in it. It says what to add. It never says what to skip.
