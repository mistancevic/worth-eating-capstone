# Decisions

One entry per product decision. What the options were, which one was taken, why,
and what evidence would reverse it. This is the file that answers "how did you
come to that conclusion" six months later, when the chat that produced it is
gone.

Older decisions are scattered through `develop.md` and the commit history. They
will be pulled in here over time. New ones start here.

---

## 2026-09-17 · The pasta night is the moment the product is shown through

**Options.** Two scenes from `journeys.md`, both real to the data:

1. The 21:35 feeding: 50 g of Vollkornbrot and 40 g of Gouda carry 13.5 g of
   protein against a 26 g meal trigger. He has eaten, feels he has eaten, and the
   day moves from 33 g to 46 g. The failure hides because it does not feel like
   skipping a meal.
2. The pasta night: work runs long, the children are served first, and by his
   turn he picks at what is left on their plates. Same arithmetic, 33 g to 46 g,
   but as a scene with people in it.

**Taken.** The pasta night.

**Why.** PlateMate's final feedback made a third of the grade about delivery and
named what earned it: a specific moment, a decision the viewer has actually
faced, one hard number, then the contrast with a stop. The pasta night has all
of that and the 21:35 feeding has only the number. Nobody has stood at a counter
weighing 40 g of Gouda; everybody with children has eaten off their plate. The
console built under Prompt 10 onward is shaped around this scene.

**What would reverse it.** Pass 3 found that fathers report eating late but do
not report cooking for the family as the reason they cannot manage their own
food; that framing was almost entirely mothers'. If the first interviews with
S2 fathers do not recognise the scene, the moment moves back to the 21:35
feeding, which is segment-neutral, and the product story loses people but keeps
its number. Recorded in `journeys.md` as an open question on Tom's second pain
point.

**Where it lands.** `docs/decisions.md` (here), the Prompt 10 build note in
`develop.md`, and the video outline when Section D is reached.
