# Discovery

Project Idea Grill, then the Discovery walkthrough. One question at a time.

Nothing is filled in until it is settled.

## The ten questions

1. Who is the specific user?
2. What event starts the workflow?
3. What does the user do today?
4. What part of the workflow should the agent handle?
5. What should the agent not be allowed to do?
6. What synthetic data will you use?
7. What will the prototype visibly show?
8. What are five eval cases?
9. What makes this agentic rather than just a chatbot?
10. Can this be built in a small prototype?
